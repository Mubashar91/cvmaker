# Research behind the 20 keyword-focused guides

Sources checked: **9 November 2026**. This is an editorial research record, not an external endorsement or a claim that a careers service reviewed Folio.

The research pass used government career services, university careers teams, a hiring-system vendor, and primary technical documentation. It preceded the revisions described below. Publisher pages were read directly where accessible; the MIT AI, CareerOneStop formatting, and Job Bank material was also available through the publishers' indexed text when direct requests were limited. Unread or unrelated search results were not used as evidence.

## Practical editing pass

The subsequent content pass tightened all 20 introductions and added a direct answer, an original worked example, a completion checklist, and three editorially selected next reads to every post. The additions apply the researched principles to fictional scenarios; they are not quotations, measured hiring outcomes, or additional case studies from the referenced organisations.

Examples now address concrete decisions: keeping a portable draft, distinguishing PDF output from editing data, classifying missing keywords, connecting an activity to a skill, explaining a project's contribution, and adapting a country-specific application. The article template links directly to the example and checklist and avoids repeating the generic editor prompt after a more relevant tool link.

## Product checks and boundaries

Folio-specific statements were checked against the editor, context, PDF, keyword, and writing-tool implementation. Those statements describe the current local product: manual browser saving, portable JSON drafts, A4 CV PDF output, and deterministic browser tools. External career sources do not establish these product capabilities.

All worked examples remain fictional and were written for these articles. No source establishes an interview guarantee, a universal ATS passing score, or a fixed CV length for every employer. Advice was scoped to the relevant country or role, and source guidance was distinguished from Folio's practical applications.

This pass researched factual content. Search volume, keyword difficulty, live rankings, and Search Console performance were not measured. The keyword list remains a set of candidates, as recorded in `SEO-STRATEGY.md`.
